#CAS Applied Data Science – Module 6: Deep Learning & Final Project Work
<p>This module contains materials and the schedule for Module 6 of the CAS Applied Data Science program at the University of Bern.

<p>Module 6 focuses on deep learning, especially feed-forward neural networks, optimization, regularization, and hands-on tutorials. The week blends lectures, practical work, and social activities, including skiing and an evening excursion.</p>
